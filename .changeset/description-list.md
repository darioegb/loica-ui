New `DescriptionList`, `DescriptionTerm` and `DescriptionDetails`, on the native `dl`, `dt` and `dd`: the fields of a record or the facts of a project. `layout` puts each detail under its term (`stacked`) or beside it, with the terms in a column as wide as the longest one (`inline`), and `size` scales the text and the space. The terms are muted, the details have no margin, and `variant="unstyled"` drops the styles.
