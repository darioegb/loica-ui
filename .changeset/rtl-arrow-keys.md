The arrow keys follow the direction of the text. In right-to-left text (`dir="rtl"` or the CSS `direction`), the left arrow goes to the next tab, dot, box or day and opens a `Tree` branch, and the right arrow goes the other way, in `Tabs`, `Tree`, the `Carousel` dots, `PinInput` and the calendar of the `DatePicker`. The arrows of the `Paginator`, the `Carousel`, the `Tree` and the calendar are mirrored, and the days of the calendar use logical margins.
