`Text` and `Heading` take a `weight` (`normal`, `medium`, `semibold` or `bold`, from the theme), and `size="inherit"` makes them take their font size from the element around them, so a title inside a component with its own size scales with it. `Text` can also be a `dd`, a `dt` or an `li`, so a definition list needs no wrapper.
